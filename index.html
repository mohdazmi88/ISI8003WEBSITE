Layman's Guide & Search Protocol: How to Ace Your Article Reviews

1. Where & What to Search (Google Scholar)

Go to Google Scholar (scholar.google.com) and find a published peer-reviewed academic journal article. Use targeted search keywords like:

[islamic science] [islamic philosophy of science] [islamic bioethics]

2. Highlight the Gaps & Limitations

Do not merely summarize or praise the article! A critical review means dissecting what the author missed, oversimplified, or failed to address. Identify weak assumptions, secular biases, or methodological gaps.

🎯
Lecturer's Direct Expectation

"I need your opinion — your opinion is what counts!"

Stand your ground intellectually. Don't be shy to challenge the author's conclusions using sound Islamic epistemic logic. We want your analytical voice, critical stance, and original philosophical assessment.

Required Critical Review Anatomy
1. Thesis & Scope Summary of central arguments, methodologies, and core hypotheses.
2. Axiological Assumptions Underlying philosophical worldview, secular vs tawhidic premises.
3. Scholarly Critique & Voice Your independent evaluation of gaps, blind spots, and counter-perspectives.
